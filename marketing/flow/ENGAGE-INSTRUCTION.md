# Daily engagement round (Claude in Chrome, about 15 minutes)

**Why:** comments on big accounts are where strangers find us. A sharp comment early on a post with thousands of
viewers is seen by more people than our own posts right now. Following on its own does little; **commenting is
the reach.**

**The rule that keeps the account safe:** Instagram bans automated comments and follows ("inauthentic activity")
and action-blocks accounts that do them. So the agent **finds the posts and writes the comments**, and **Jabir
presses Post and Follow himself.** The agent never presses them. Your taps are what make it allowed.

**Your part:**
1. Open Chrome, logged in to instagram.com as @azmin_digital.
2. Open the Claude side panel and paste the one line below.
3. Read each drafted comment, change anything you like, and press **Post** yourself.

Tip: if your Claude extension has **Shortcuts** (saved prompts), save the line as a shortcut named `engage`, so
next time you just type `/engage`.

## Agent steps

```
You are preparing today's Instagram engagement round for @azmin_digital (Jabir, Dubai): scroll-film websites for
property, cars and Dubai brands, built with AI + code. Goal: be seen by the right people through useful comments.

HARD LIMITS (never break these):
- NEVER press "Post" on a comment, NEVER press "Follow", NEVER like, NEVER send a DM. Jabir presses Post/Follow.
- Never mention our page, our services, prices, links, "DM me" or "check my profile" in a comment. No pitching.
- Max 10 comments and max 10 follow suggestions per day.
- Read only what you need: today's accounts below, latest 1-2 posts each. No mass scrolling, no data collection.
- If Instagram shows a warning, a captcha, "try again later" or an action block: STOP and tell Jabir.

1. TODAY'S ACCOUNTS
   Open https://github.com/Mjabir07/azmin-instagram-growth/blob/main/marketing/engage/targets.md
   Take today's rotation (by weekday, Dubai time) and the "every day" peers listed there.

2. PICK THE POSTS
   For each account, open its profile and its newest post or reel. Use it only if it was posted in the last
   48 hours (the earlier we comment, the more people see it). Skip ads, giveaways and posts with comments turned off.
   Aim for 8 to 10 posts in total.

3. WRITE ONE COMMENT PER POST (the ig-comment rules)
   - 1 to 3 short sentences, in plain English. One idea. Say something specific to THIS post.
   - Pick the right type: add a fact or number, add the missing case, ask one real question, share a short
     experience ("we built a launch page and..."), respectful disagreement, or a short true one-liner.
     For property and car posts, our natural angle is the buyer's experience on the phone: how the listing, launch
     page or booking feels on mobile, Arabic + English, payment plans, booking on WhatsApp.
   - Never start with: Great post, Love this, So true, Amazing, Needed this, Wow, the creator's name + "!", or an emoji.
   - No emoji-only comments. No hashtags. No em dashes (—). No words like: elevate, unlock, game-changer,
     next level, revolutionary, seamless, delve.
   - Never restate the post. Never flatter. Never pitch.
   - If you can't say something genuinely useful about a post, skip it.

4. PUT EACH COMMENT IN PLACE (don't post)
   For each post, open it in its own tab, click the comment box and type the comment, but DO NOT press Post.
   Leave the tabs open in order.

5. FOLLOW SUGGESTIONS
   Suggest up to 10 new accounts from today's group that we don't follow yet (active in the last 30 days, real
   businesses or people). Don't press Follow.

6. REPORT (in this chat)
   A table: # | account | post (1 line) | posted (hours ago) | comment type | the comment text.
   Then the follow suggestions as: handle | followers | why.
   End with: "Read each tab, edit if you like, and press Post yourself. Tell me which follows you want and press
   Follow yourself."
```
