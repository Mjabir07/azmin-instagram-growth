## Who we are

- **Name:** Azmin Digital
- **Handle:** @azmin_digital
- **What we do, in one sentence:** We build scroll-driven websites and apps that sell property and supercars, plus AI brand ambassadors like Yara.
- **Who we're talking to:** Off-plan developers, brokerages and property marketing heads in Dubai and the UAE; supercar rental, dealership and detailing owners in the GCC.
- **What we sell:** Launch microsites and full websites (from a few weeks), booking and enquiry systems, mobile apps, and AI ambassadors (a consistent face and voice for a brand's videos).

## What we sound like

- **Voice on the reels:** Yara, our openly AI ambassador. Warm, calm, confident; a consultant who knows the market, not an influencer.
- **On camera:** faceless website showcases narrated by Yara, plus Yara-led talking reels.
- **Words we use:** your buyers, your launch, your fleet, scroll, film, floor plan, payment plan, book, WhatsApp, Arabic and English.
- **Words we never say:** game-changer, revolutionary, elevate, unlock, next level, best in Dubai, guaranteed, limited time only.
- **Swearing:** no.
- **Emoji in captions:** one at most, rarely.
- **Pace:** about 165 words per minute (Yara's narration).

## Our positions

1. A launch PDF loses buyers; a site they scroll through keeps them.
2. Arabic is not a translation job at the end, it's half the market from day one.
3. Buyers decide on their phone, so the phone version is the real website.
4. Fewer, deeper niches beat "we build websites for everyone".
